The connective tissue

Ragguagli — the conversion rates

A ragguaglio is the heart of this notebook: a rule for turning a weight, a length, a measure or a coin in one marketplace into its exact equivalent in another. They are what let a Florentine merchant buy in Avignon, weigh in Barcelona, and sell in Alexandria without being cheated.

How to read this page. We re-read every leaf that records a rate and turned it into a number, so the book can be checked against itself. Most weight rates pivot on the Barcelona quintale, so we start there. Then you can convert between any two cities the book connects, and see where its figures agree across leaves and where they diverge — a useful signal both of the trade's real variation and of our reading's uncertainty. Figures are a best-effort reading of a dense hand; treat divergences as questions, not verdicts.
