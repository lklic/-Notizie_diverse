**Interpretive note.** A wool-buyer's reference page for the Spanish / western-Mediterranean trade. The opening paragraph surveys the wool-producing districts of the Valencia–Murcia region (Castellón=*Chastighone*, *villa reale*=Villarreal, Almenara, *murya*=Murcia, Castile=*ghastella*), grading their fleeces as bad, coarse and *ramose*, or as quite good of the *morsorte* (medium) staple. The body is a price list of individual lots: each line gives a place, a quantity (mostly in hundreds/thousands, marked `lb°`/`p(er)º`), a quality term (*buone*, *ghattive*, *ghommali*, *bonsime*), and the value/duty "delivered" (*adporto*) at Valencia or Pampalona, quoted in soldi (14–20). The penultimate entry sums up "all 13 places of the Campo di Monteagudo(?)" of the Valle di Navarra. Many proper names remain doubtful (see `[?]`), and a brown stain obscures parts of the lower middle.
