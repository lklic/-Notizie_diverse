# Image 003 — Folio 1r (title page / opening leaf)

*(Title page of the volume. The recto is blank apart from a two-line heading written near the top, set in from the left margin. The top line is a small register/numbering mark "ij·xxij·" with a superscript "o"; below it the ownership title in mercantesca cursive, ending in a long pen flourish. A faint light-pencil foliation mark ("c") sits in the upper outer (right) corner. The rest of the leaf is blank, showing strong bleed-through of text from the following pages, scattered foxing/stains, and a trimmed/worn outer edge with the next leaf's index tabs visible toward the fore-edge.)*

**English translation.**

> *(register mark)* ij · 22 ·
>
> ※ This book · X · of Stoldo · di Lionardo · Frescobaldi

*(The heading identifies the volume: "This book X of Stoldo di Lionardo Frescobaldi" — i.e. Book X belonging to Stoldo, son of Lionardo Frescobaldi. The "ij·xxij·" above is an inventory/register number with a superscript "o". The bracketed "c" in the corner is a later/modern foliation note.)*

---

**Interpretive note.** This is the opening title leaf of the Frescobaldi merchant notebook, bearing the ownership inscription "Questo libro X di Stoldo di Lionardo Frescobaldi" (Florence, c. 1417–1429). Apart from this heading and a small register number, the page is otherwise blank; the trade content (wool and spice gazetteers, ragguagli, etc.) begins on the following leaves, whose index tabs are visible at the outer edge.
