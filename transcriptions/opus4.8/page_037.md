(Heading) *Here we shall record at Genoa (Genova) the customs, measures and weights according to the merchant Sibandino [a Genoese factor] at Genoa.*

- One records *soldo cberudo*(?) … the *barili* [casks] of … 7. A barrel makes one Neapolitan *botte* [cask].
- One records wine … and they are barrels; one [is a] Neapolitan [measure]; … the barrels are one *mezzeruola*; they are weighed not…
- One records grain at … which are lb …; 1[…] 280 di Genova [makes a] *lamina* coarse / coarse [grade].

**Interpretive note.** This page is a dedicated gazetteer/handbook section on **Genoa (Genova)**, opening with a heading attributing the information to a Genoese merchant/factor ("Sibandino"). It records how various commodities (wine in casks, grain, cloths, silks, spices, gold thread, leather) are measured, weighed and sold at Genoa, with notes on the local consular/weigher (*almotacén*) customs and duties. Its core is a weight-conversion table showing how **100 libbre of Genoa** convert into the pounds of fourteen marketplaces (Palermo, Pisa, Bologna, Florence, Milan, Avignon, Montpellier, Barcelona, Seville, Paris, Venice, Naples, Rome, Caffa), followed by silver/spice equivalences with Alexandria and a series of *ogno/alma* oil(?)-measure ragguagli with Florence, Rome, Marseille and Seville.
