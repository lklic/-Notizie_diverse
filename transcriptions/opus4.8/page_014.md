**Interpretive note.** A ragguaglio (cross-market conversion) leaf reconciling the weights, measures and money of account of Venice (Vinegra/Vinegia) with Barcelona — libbra sottile, oncia, the "marscio"/peso, the cask (botte), and the grosso/reale, with side notes on freight (nolo), the decima (tithe/duty) and trading percentages — and a parallel block doing the same for Naples and Gaeta (the tarì / grani / tornese coinage and the quintaro). The final block is a short calendar of southern-Italian trade fairs (Gaeta, Salerno, etc.) with opening and closing dates in September–March. Many internal figures and verbs are read with low confidence owing to abrasion and dense abbreviation.
