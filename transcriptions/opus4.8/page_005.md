**Interpretive note.** A Barcelona / Catalonia commercial gazetteer in mercantesca cursive. The upper block records exchange ragguagli for cloth/yarn goods (Milanese, Como, Brescia, Florentine) that "convert in Barcelona" or "in the said place," quoted in *robe* / *rotoli* and *once*. A short travel/lodging block follows (Tarascona, Belghama, Avignon) with entry-and-lodging charges in *soldi* and *denari*. The lower block is a price-and-duty list of spices and trade goods sold at Barcelona — wax, dates, saffron(?), camlet, galls, and others — each with a quantity (up to N *t°*), a quality term (buona / ghattissimo / comunali), a tare ("pesa av. 2"), and the customs duty or selling charge ("sporto / dssesa … a usanza"). A bottom brace ties four Romagna/Catalan place names to a note on "very bad wools" sold by balance-weight. The names of Matteo da Barcellona and Avignon tie these notes to the Frescobaldi western-Mediterranean trade. Many proper and commodity names are abraded and read uncertainly.
