**Interpretive note.** A double-section market gazetteer for the Iberian / western-Mediterranean trade, covering Seville (Cibilia/Siviglia) and Montpellier (Monolieri). For each marketplace the scribe lists what is sold there (gold and tin in *piastre*, silver alloyed to a stated *denari* fineness, Barbary silver wares, spices, wool, fustians, taffetà, alum, lead, saltpetre), how the local weight and money convert back to Florence (Seville → Florence lb 126; Montpellier → Florence lb 140; reckonings in *maravedís* and *doblas*, with 36 maravedís = 1 dobla), and the local sales-tax / toll regime (a "Nota" giving rates of 5%, 2⅓% and 1% depending on the goods, with notes on what is and is not exempt). The closing "✠ casi d…" lines in each section enumerate the export commodities flowing from Seville/Lisbon and from Montpellier. Many trailing words are abraded and several commodity/quality terms are read with reservation.
