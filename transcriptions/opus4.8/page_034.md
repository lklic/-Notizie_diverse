**Interpretive note.** A silver **assay table** continuing the coinage material of the surrounding leaves. The first block rates silver coins — *grossi* (Ferrara, Genova, Provenza, Venezia), *bolognini* (Bologna/Ferrara/Genova), the *ghontoni[?]* of Ancona and Urbino, *reali* of Barcelona and Spain, and *carlini* of Naples and Venice — by their fineness in once and denari per pound (12 once = fine silver). The second block, under the rubric "for goldsmiths," gives the silver standard city by city across Italy and the transalpine markets (Firenze, Siena, Bologna, Milano, Genova, Provenza, Parigi/Fiandra, Inghilterra, Catalogna/Spagna, Napoli/Gaeta). The third block, "how silver is sold," with its dense closing prose, records the selling standards and deductions for Florence, Venice, Rome, Naples and Bologna. Several coin-names and the final lines remain doubtful and are marked `[?]`.
