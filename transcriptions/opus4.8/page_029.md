**Interpretive note.** This page combines a shipping/packing record with a commodity reference. The left column lists two bales of dyed broadcloths (*drappi/panni*) by colour — scarlets, blues, turquoise, whites, greys, violets, purples, greens — dispatched from Pisa overseas in 1419, with their per-dozen valuations and a freight/customs note (the forwarding place-name read here as *Carpiza* is uncertain). The right column is a merchant's qualitative price-list for assorted wares: woollen/cotton-brass caps (*berretti/barini*, including London- and Rome-made), wax, brass thread and cords from Milan, gums, ambergris and rhubarb, with quality grades and prices in florins, soldi and per-pound rates. The cursive is heavily abbreviated; many right-column readings, especially commodity-names abutting the trimmed edge, remain uncertain.
