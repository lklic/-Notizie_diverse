# Image 055 — Folio 27r

*(Two-column page; original folio number "27" inked in the top outer (right) corner. A table of exchange usances: each line gives a route from one banking-place to a destination, the term of the bill ("vista" = days after sight, or "fatta" = payable at the fair-date, or "mesi due/tre" = 2/3 months) and on whose account the cost falls — the recurring closing words read as two alternating forms, here "s'à padovese" and "s'à pancese" (the loss/charge falls to one party or the other). The page is organised by origin: the left column runs §[firenze] then §pisa; the right column runs +firenze (continued), +Genova, +Napoli. Light brown foxing/stains mid-page; ink clear and legible throughout.)*

---

**Interpretive note.** A reference table of exchange usances (*tempi/usanze della lettera del cambio*) for bills of exchange drawn from the major banking-places of Florence, Pisa, Genoa, and Naples to a wide network of markets — Barcelona, Avignon, Montpellier, Valencia, Paris, Bruges, London, Normandy, Seville, Lyon, Milan, Rome, Perugia, Bologna, Naples/Gaeta/Castello, Palermo, Messina, and "Alemagna" (Germany). For each route it records the customary maturity (so many days after sight, or payable at the fair-date, or two/three months) and on whose account the exchange charge falls, with several reciprocal return terms noted. It is a working reference for merchant-bankers calculating the maturities and value-dates of their drafts.
