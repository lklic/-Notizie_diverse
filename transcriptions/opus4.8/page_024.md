**Interpretive note.** This dense page is a block of *ragguagli* (mercantile equivalences and freight/charge schedules) centred on the wool trade between **Valencia / Catalonia / Barcelona** and **Florence**, with comparison to **Pisan** and **English** wool (priced per mark in the English *noble*), plus weight standards from **Germany**, barrel/cask capacities of **Genova** and **Flanders**, salt *moggio* conversions (Aigues-Mortes), and itemized loading/sea-freight expenses (*spese*) via the **port of Pisa** and **Avignon/Provence**. It records how sealed bales (*tovaglie*) and net wool weights "in verga" convert from Valencian/Catalan pounds to Florentine pounds (lb 120→125; lb 150→157; net 190–192) and lists charges in soldi and denari.
