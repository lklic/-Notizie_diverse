**English translation.**

> **+ Granata (Granada) with Barcelona — fol. 16**

- One hundred *cane* of Granada of the *carradori* converts at Barcelona to between 157 and 155 *cane*.
- And likewise, of *palmi* 10 per *cana*, and the *cana* of Granada makes 9 *palmi*.
- And 100 *donne* (a measure) of Granada are at Barcelona 175 *donne*, and the *cana* of Granada at Barcelona = 1 7/20 *cana*.
- And 100 *libbre* of Granada convert at Barcelona to ... 3 and 11 ... 19 ½ and ½ d.; of Granada they are at Barcelona 1 lb [pound].
- The "archassa" (skein/hank) of spun gold, being 60 [parts] of gold, converts at Barcelona to 54 *once* [ounces].
- Per *libbra* of ... of Granada it converts at Barcelona to 10 2/4 *once* of *monete* 9, [reckoned] in *moneta* of balance; and of Granada 9 *once*, with a charge (difesa) of 2 per ...
- The merchandise of Genoa here at Barcelona [weighs] 1 ½ *barre*, of which of 56 and one *tocco* converts at Barcelona to 63 lb, and the *libro* with charge in itself ... in the name of Barcelona, from 20 first, by proportion.
- Nine and a third *once* of Granada make one *moueḡo* (measure) at Barcelona.
- The *quintale* of wool (*lana*?) at Barcelona, [its] basis (?) reckoned per first 190 *libbre* net and ..., and the merchandise of balance reckoned for Granada, and the charge reckoned for Granada, brokerage (*holuolo*) and provision of Granada, conducted in itself without insurance, 6 ⅓ per first.
- *Quadrelime* (?) ... charge reckoned for Granada in the said manner, 10 per first.
- And spun gold at charge reckoned for Granada in itself, opening of ship first and ... and the "sgassa" [unloading?]; and at Barcelona one returns the *quintali grossi* ... [for] *cane*; ... of one, and one Barcelonese "archassa".
- Sfax (*Ffaculo*) goes for [via] Montpellier ... at the charge for Granada and the expense of return, from 6 ⅓ of ½ per first of Granada.
- Per the ... of *marcholi* (?) in itself a half: from 62 and 62 per first per first of Granada.

> **+ Palermo with Barcelona**

- The *onza* of *garbino* [is] 30 *tarì*, the *tarì* worth 2 *carlini*, and the *carlino* [is] 10 *grani*; the *durbato* (ducat?) worth 12 *garbini* and one *aspro* besides.
- The *cantaro* of Palermo is 1 ½ *cantara* of Barcelona, and the *padno* (cloth?) of Barcelona [is] one *cana*; 12 *cadrara rosata* (?), 1 lb per *pesa*.
- The *quintali* at Palermo [is] 100 lb, and *quintali* 1 ⅗ of Barcelona [equal] one *coverga* of 12 lb of Barcelona.

> **+ Charges as they are laid out**

- For "foresi" (gate/measuring duty), measuring: of *forspoy* (?) 4 *grani*, and the *lattadno* (the recipient?) 3 *grani* per *pezza*.
- For brokerage (*sensaria*): of *forspoy* 10 *grani*, of the *lattadno* 9 *grani*.
- For "foresi" to remove (?) *arbasa* (rough cloth) 9 *grani* per *balla*.
- For the duty (*duretto* = diritto) of the *cantaro* and per first of *forspoy* and other duties not ..., according to how each falls ... of such and [other] duties.

**Interpretive note.** This page (fol. 16r) is a *ragguagli* and metrology sheet for the market of **Barcelona**. The first and longest block converts the cloth and weight measures (*cana*, *palmo*, *donna/donne*, *libbra*, *quintale*) of **Granada** against those of Barcelona and Genoa, and lists the customary charges (*difesa*, brokerage *holuolo*, provision, insurance) on wool, spun gold (*oro filato*) and merchandise, with notes on routing via **Sfax (Ffaculo)** and **Montpellier**. A middle block sets the coinage and weights of **Palermo** (*onza*, *tarì*, *carlino*, *grano*, *garbino*, *cantaro*, *quintale*) against Barcelona, and a final short block lists the gate/brokerage/cantaro **duties** (foresi, sensaria, diritto). Many internal numerals and abbreviated terms are read with low confidence.
