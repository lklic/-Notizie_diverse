**English translation.**

**+ Mallorca, for the same [continued]**

- ※ As [the wools] are made on the island of Mallorca, they amount altogether to about [c.] cantara …4(?); the quantity, all told, that there is in Mallorca about quantity 4900.
- **+ Following, the places of the wool [districts].**
- ※ Andratx(?), Calvià(?), Perpunyent(?), purged: these three places are in the mountains, and on the mountain slope they lie near[?]; and these are the finest wools, the best-classed of the island; they make about quantity 1,000.
- ※ Bunyola(?), Bellver(?), Manacor(?): these three places make about 1,000 [units], good wools, but they are not as perfect as those above.
- ※ [a long list of places — Stanyol, Porreres, l'Apomoua(?), Albuçaix(?), Montuïri, Pollença, Sineu …] — make coarse wool, Porreres, Santa Margarita(?), … Pollença, Muro(?); all these places generally yield [their wool]; it is coarse and earthy [grosse e charcgnie e terra], the wools are sweated[?]; one must beware of much mixing, as the very coarse [wool] is most mingled with that of the mountains, very coarse, and first[?].
- ※ Whence sir, the thing sorted weighs lb. 104 of salamone(?); these come at a weight of about lb. 100; and likewise(?) there are large lots [chargiory] of mortma(?); these are sorted, bought from the tree(?), at 13 d. per 12; they usually are worth lb. 4 to 6 the dozen.
- ※ The "ontaly" [lamb-wool] are very … lamb-wools, and they are milk[-lambs?] from March to May, legally regulated; they usually are worth, of one, lb. 5; sold at 12 d. per 13.
- For "astrogma"(?) they are skins of little lambs about 6 months old; they come [to market] thus and so; and these full lamb-skins, the dozen is worth lb. 8 to 12 in Mallorca, of one.

**+ Likewise Mallorca**

- ※ Whence sir, likewise the … weighs lb. 9 oz., and net of the … forty[?] within; it bears a cask of Mallorca[-measure].
- ※ Fornalutx(?), Bunyola(?), Alaró, Selva, Valldemossa, and Esporles(?), Fosi(?), in the mountains [montantaggniah], likewise they are … apart; sorted commonly at lb. 2 d. 8 per … ; and likewise classed, placed [in] Mallorca.
- ※ Andratx, Oglina(?), Puigpunyent, Pollença(?), and in Campos[-of-the-]border, and these are in the flat[lands] of the island, and they are not so much in demand … in the Levant.
- ※ Other places of the island not named are quite good, likewise for keeping; depending on good weather they keep a great quantity, [worth] keeping.
- ※ They are bought only at one time, around St. Bartholomew's [feast].
- ※ One reckons the charges of the places around Mallorca at 3¼ to 3⅛ … cabotte(?) or …

- ※ … below … the wools of the island are of Apulia(?)-[type]; … falls lb. 8; the others lb. 28 to 29.

**Interpretive note.** This page is a detailed gazetteer of the wool districts (*lane*) of the island of Mallorca (*Maiolicha / Maioria*), under the heading "+ Maiolicha p(er) lamedesima" and a second sub-heading "+ cosi Maiolicha." It enumerates dozens of island place-names (Andratx, Pollença, Porreres, Sineu, Montuïri, Alaró, Selva, Valldemossa, Puigpunyent, Campos, etc.) grouped into mountain districts producing the finest wools (~1,000 units) and plains/lower districts producing coarser, lesser wools, with total island production estimated around 4,900. It records qualities (fine vs. coarse/earthy "grosse e charcgnie"), lamb-wool and lambskins ("agnielli," 6-month "astrogma"), the buying season (around St. Bartholomew), sorting weights (lb. 104 / lb. 100 / lb. 9 oz.), and prices per dozen (lb. 4-6, lb. 5, lb. 8-12) — a practical wool-buyer's manual for the Mallorcan market.
