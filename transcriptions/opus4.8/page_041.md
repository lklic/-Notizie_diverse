**Interpretive note.** The folio (later-numbered 120) opens with notes on **Norway/Bruges** metrology — a Norwegian grain *staio* of 133 gross *lb* = 109 fine *lb*, saffron grades, and pearl/gold/silver assay equivalences expressed in *carati*, *once*, *denari* and *fiorini/grossi*, including gold at 23¼ ct = 16 grossi/oncia and 21⅛ ct = 15 grossi/oncia, and the note that the Norwegian ducat = 24 grossi. It then becomes a systematic **exchange (cambio/ragguaglio) gazetteer**, listing for Bologna, Florence, Mantua/Padua, Rome, Naples–Gaeta–Puglia, Milan, Avignon, Montpellier, Barcelona/Valencia, Paris, Bruges and London both how the ducat (or local money) is quoted against the Norway "*ƒ di grossi*" rate and how the reverse leg is taken, all qualified "secondo che il tempo" (according to the prevailing rate). The London entry quotes the ducat at 36–40 sterling.
