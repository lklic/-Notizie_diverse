**Interpretive note.** A *ragguagli* (commercial-equivalence) leaf comparing four marketplaces with Barcelona: Ibiza (Eviza) and Seville (Sibilia) chiefly for salt (*sale*) and its dry measures (*fanega*, *staio*) reckoned against Barcelona and Florence; San Matteo for hides and furs (*pellame*, *pene*, *cuoia*) with their charges (*spese*) and duties to Barcelona; and Valencia for assorted goods including wool (*lana*) and oil (*olio*), giving weight, measure and freight (*nolo*) conversions to Barcelona and Florence. The Ibiza and Seville salt passages are reasonably well inked but the technical vocabulary of the measures is heavily abbreviated; many internal figures and verbs remain uncertain.
