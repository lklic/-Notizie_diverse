**English translation.** (Approximate. This page lists Spanish/Aragonese-Catalan wools by district, with bale-weights, quality, the grade "terunolo" (= Teruel wool), and the freight-and-charge to Valencia. lb = libbre/pounds; on = ounces; f = fiorini/florins; d. = denari. Iberian place-names kept in original spelling and largely uncertain.)

- ※ *Sferro / al Ghastellanio*: lb 300, quite good, and it is "terunolo" (Teruel wool); shipped to Valencia at f 2.
- ※ *Ghado*: lb 300, good, and of "strech"; first it goes inland(?) then to Valencia; carried as far as Valencia at f 2¼.
- ① *Diamante* lb 400 / *Argiento* lb 700 / *Volava* twohundred lb 250 / *Paviali* lb 600 / *Poghalisos* lb 250 / and of *Agniodalida* lb 700 — all good wool and "terunolo"; first it goes to Teruel then to Valencia; shipped at a charge of f 2.
  - Of *Ghabrea* and of all the district of the Ghabra, about lb 1500 — bad (cattive) wool; first from the district [carried] to Valencia at f 2¼.
- ① *Osso* "dritto" lb 250, good, from the district, at a charge as far as Valencia, f 2.
  - *Alfa* lb 400 / *Valava Ghomede* lb 400 / *Santa Valava* lb 300 / *Torra Londerera* lb 390 / *Vollofranqa* (Villafranca?) lb 390 / *Buongiera* lb 100, common — wool ... f lb ¼; *Nora* and the district, at a charge as far as Valencia, f 2¼.
  - The wool of the Fera of the Barozzi are all good, better in quality (migliori) than the wool of the Terunna; from Teruel(?) than the greater Torra: each "g" per [...] d 26 for extra charge.
- ① *Cera / Vacca* and all their districts of Perhanpora ... is bought ... at the Boragizo, are good and better in quality than that of the Boragizo / ... is worth ... and Ferranza // and Alaona ... of Vendrele ... and of bad (cattive) [quality], especially of the Morosa of the Boto.
- ① *Borrha Osme* ... lb m gathered/coloured, good, and the "rova" lb 35 ⅔; but it is bought ... at Valencia this time.
  - ⟨ⓜ⟩ *Ola* are good, of fine wool, with much short (corte) hair, and it is rare; of *Ghalorra* and *Ellorera* lb 38 ½; but it is bought ... at Valencia ...
- ✝ Of *Ghoraggo* the Teruel [wools], of "terunolo" have much hair, not very good.

**+ Duties (Diritti):**
- ɴ At *Terunino / Teruel*, in all one pays a toll (pedaggio) for everything, lb 2.
- ɴ At the *Fera* of the Barozzino, of weight 40 ... the "soo" and per "affono" coarse.
  - ʝ In all at *Aragona* (Aragon), one pays a "commerciale" duty of 14 ... per d.
- ɴ At *El Latio of Tortosa* one pays the "ammaggaramza" and "dasigona" duty d 16 per somo (load).

**+ In the castle and district of Amolina(?):**
- ⓜ *Olina / Novega / Erea / Lugaroni / Purghera / Ghovelin / Todasl* — all are in the territory of Amolina and its castle, and all are good wool.

**+ In the mountains of Campo d'Aronza:**
- ɔ *Ife / Moinole / Mocoba / Ottoi / Piu Plenas / Mourghadella / Chulara of the Nabaros / Sferrore / Algunglo* — all are in the mountains of Campo d'Aronza; all good wool, better than the wool of Campo dell'Ofqis, and of the district of Ghuos.
- ① *Ghonsa* good / *Fimercola / Vallorret / Corsaffrora / Novaret / Consala / Gorggolio / Mainara* — are of Campo d'Aronza, good and abundant(?) wool.
- ⓜ *Orcal / Ghorggno / Odne / Puyral / Blaqueo Bello / Ogni Sneragio* — are in the territory of Morcale and of the territory of Ghahogat, and are good wool.

**Interpretive note.** A Spanish/Aragonese wool gazetteer cataloguing wool-producing localities — bale-weights in libbre, quality terms (buone, ghattde = cattive, mighory = migliori), the marketable grade "terunolo" (Teruel wool), and the freight/charge (f 2, f 2¼) to ship them to "avalonza" (Valencia). A central "Diritti" rubric records the tolls and commercial duties (pedaggio, commerciale, ammaggaramza) payable at Teruel, the Barozzino fair, Aragon, and Tortosa. The lower half groups individual wool villages under three district headings: the castle/territory of "Amolina", the mountains of "Campo d'Aronza", and the territory of "Morcale/Ghahogat". Most micro-toponyms are obscure Iberian place-names read with low confidence.
