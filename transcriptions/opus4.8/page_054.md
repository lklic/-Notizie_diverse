---

**Interpretive note.** The upper half is a merchant's gazetteer of the saffron (*zaffrana*) fairs of Provence and Languedoc — Montpellier, Carpentras, Monteux and others feeding the Avignon market — with each fair's calendar (Pentecost lasting eight days before and after, Our Lady of September, All Saints in November, a mid-June fair lasting the whole month, one at St Andrew's, etc.), size and goods. The lower half is an unrelated Florentine price-list of silk fabrics — taffeta, plain velvet and damask in crimson, black and "alessandrino" blue — each with a base price and a higher "al tempo" price, taffeta sold by the pound and the velvets/damasks by the braccio, all in fiorini. The proper nouns and several verbs of the saffron section are abraded and read uncertainly.
