**Interpretive note.** A ragguagli (comparative-market) leaf converting the weights, measures and money of Bruges (Flanders) and of Majorca ("Maiolicha") into those of Barcelona. It equates the libbra and quintale of each market (e.g. lb 95 of Bruges = 1 quintar of Barcelona; quintar of 100 lb = 117 at Barcelona), gives the value of the grosso coin, and lists charges (freight by land vs. sea, customs/colta, commission/surgunta) on goods such as cotton (bombasi), quicksilver/archavecha[?], wax (cera) and tin (stagni). Money is expressed in soldi (ß), denari (d.) and grossi, including local Barcelonese currency. Heavily abbreviated mercantesca; figures mostly legible but many commodity names and verbs read with low confidence.
