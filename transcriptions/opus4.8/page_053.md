**English translation.**

**+ Constantinople**
- In Constantinople two weights (peso) are used: the cantaro grosso (heavy) and the cantaro sottile (light).
- The cantaro grosso is 140 light pounds (lb).
- The cantaro grosso is used for wax.
- The cantaro sottile is used for [mastore?].
- Silk is weighed by the light pound.
- One "albero" is understood to be [...] cantara, two, in Genoa.
- Said first heavy [cantaro] of 140 light lb converts in Genoa to 160 lb.
- And it converts in Florence to 145 lb.

**+ Kingdom of Granada [?]**
- From Granada are exported: [gherbellony?] cloths dyed red; sugars; much powder; new cloth, hides, silks, almonds, raisins (zibibbo) and many figs.
- One sources [there] sparrowhawks(?) of every kind; cargoes from Florence worth [d.] 95 and 90 [...]; iron; cargoes from Granada; fustians; and coarse paper.

**+ Valencia**
- Valencia uses for all its dealings the Castilian [system] and [its own] money.
- Proceeding-money from Genoa, from 18 to 24; in private for one's own business it is customary to deal [thus].

**+ Fairs of [the ports?]**
- Genoa trades for cash in September / toward Alexandria / [Chioggia?] / and for cloth and [...] and their wines.
- Germany (Lamagna) also trades in July, and from one [month] until September, for many cargoes of goods... on the galleys that depart there for Romania / Syria / and Alexandria.
- Germany likewise takes(?) doubloons (doppie), for galleys, from [Flanders / Frondia]... cosa.
- Rome [...] between the Pope and all year, often and late, because they will wish [...].
- From there the Pope departs every year, likewise [...].
- Naples and Gaeta: trade in wares(?) from August until [+] September for the fair of Sant'Angelo (San Gelo), and likewise for [...].
- Naples and Gaeta: [goods] come in November for [...] of theirs.
- Barcelona: likewise one buys, from one [month] until August of [...] for their [wares], and in July for grana (kermes dye); also [goods] come in October for [gaffarani/saffron?].
- Valencia: likewise in July and August for their grana (kermes dye) and figs.

---

**Interpretive note.** A regional commercial gazetteer giving, by marketplace, the local weight systems and exchange-conversions (Constantinople's cantaro grosso/sottile and its ragguaglio to Genoa and Florence pounds), then the export commodities of the Kingdom of Granada (dyed cloth, sugar, hides, silk, almonds, raisins, figs, iron, fustians, paper) and the monetary practice at Valencia. The lower block is a calendar of trading fairs and seasonal buying windows (Genoa, Germany, Rome/the papal court, Naples-Gaeta, Barcelona, Valencia), noting which months goods move and for which products (grana/kermes dye, figs, etc.). Several proper nouns and abbreviations in the "fiere" block are abraded and read tentatively.
