**Interpretive note.** The page has two parts. The upper block records the schedule of galley/cog ("chocha") voyages between Flanders ("la Magnia") and Alexandria, giving departure and return dates (25 October, 7 September; returning November–December–January), and noting cargoes of the commune, cash carried, and merchandise brought back. The lower block is a memorandum ("Salute di Stoldo") of how various commodities are bought at London ("elondruo") in the year 1415: gums, oil, hides, wax, Florentine and country woollen cloths (priced in florins per piece by grade), saffron, ginger, cinnamon, sulphur, orpiment, copper, tin/lead, salt and customs ("cabella") — each with its unit (per centonaro/hundredweight, per pizza/piece, per pound) and price or rate. Several spice/metal readings are tentative owing to heavy abbreviation.
