**English translation.**

✛ **Perpignan with Barcelona**

- The quintale of Perpignan, by hand(?), [equals] that of Barcelona, lb 1 ¼ [pounds].
- Woollen cloths of Perpignan are bought at s. [soldi] per canna [length], and so much each [...] ₣ 3 ½(?). [If] one buys [...] ₣ 18, the bolt finished(?) ₣ 60, which is s. 33 ⟨or, if you wish, for 15 over the coarse wool ₣ 4 and Bologna [cloth] ₣ 30, no — ₣ 18, which is⟩ [...] at ₣ 30 the wool comes to s. 33.
- The yearly charge on these same cloths: exported from Perpignan ¼ percent; thereafter at [Bigione?] ¾ and 6, and then washing(?), the two included, are cleaned/trimmed.
- The yearly charge on [the men/porters?] at Barcelona, and [other] charge, "hondolo": ₣ ¾–½ per canna [...] all the charges, except service ₣ 1 s. 2, that is, about ₣ 1 ½ per piece, if they have been sold in France [...] all together ₣ 2 ⅛ to 2 ¼ per [...] per cloth.

**Interpretive note.** A ragguagli (exchange-of-measures) page giving the weight, length and monetary equivalences for the woollen-cloth trade between Perpignan, Montpellier and Barcelona, with onward sale into France and references to the Catalan fairs, Bologna and Palermo. It records canna prices, percentage charges (export duty, brokerage, service, warehouse/porterage), conversions of grain prices (rye/segale, wheat/fromento) in soldi, and coin/weight reductions (libbre grosse vs. sottili, the quintale, tournois vs. fiorini). The hand is small and very abbreviated; the lower third of the leaf is blank.
