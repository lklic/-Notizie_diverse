**Interpretive note.** This is a wool gazetteer (*ragguaglio delle lane*): a systematic list of mostly Catalan/Aragonese supplying towns, recording for each the quantity put out in libbre, a quality grade (*buone* good, *sommonali* medium, *chattive* bad), how it weighs out (*pesono ave* / *asommo*), and the export charge "a Pisa" in soldi/denari per *sommo*. Groups of towns are braced to share a single note, and the closing entries cross-reference one another ("just as much as Villa fremosa"). The many obscure, phonetically spelled place-names and the tight repeating formula mark this as a merchant's procurement/pricing reference for the Catalan wool trade routed through Pisa.
