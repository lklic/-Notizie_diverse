**Interpretive note.** A *ragguagli* page of weight/measure equivalences for three western and Levantine entrepôts — **Bruges**, **London** and **Alexandria of Egypt** — each block opened by a cross-headed rubric. The short Bruges and London sections list the chief commodities of Flanders and England (cloths of Brussels and Malines, fine wools, furs, English sterling, tin/brass, alum, cottons, spices) with cloth/wool measures converted to Flemish *braccia* and Genoese units. The large Alexandria block tabulates how the Alexandrian *sporta* and *cantaro* of spices and wool convert into the pounds of Flanders, Romania, Messina, Constantinople, Naples and Provence; several of its lines are struck through. The hand is abraded and heavily abbreviated, and the trimmed line-ends and many proper names remain conjectural.
