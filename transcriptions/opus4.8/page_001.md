# Image 001

*(Front flyleaf / title page. Heavy water-staining and foxing across the lower two-thirds of the leaf; sewing-station holes run along the inner (gutter) margin, with fold creases and small repairs. A thin vertical strip/tab (place-marker) sits at mid-page. All writing is confined to the upper third in a later — c. 18th-century — cursive italic cataloguing hand (NOT the mercantesca of the body), giving a descriptive title and shelf-number for the volume. A small pen mark resembling a "+"/tab appears in the lower-right quadrant.)*

---

```
                              1409  ‖ Comodo di Ritrovare questo Libro
        Libro N.° 22  Spogliato

        Notizie diverse raccolte da Stoldo Frescobaldi
   dell' Esercizio della Mercatura circa i generi,
        pesi, misure, valore, e Ragguagli d'una Piazza
        all' Altra
```

---

**English translation.**

(top-right note) `1409 ‖ Convenient [means / index] for finding this Book`

- Book No. 22 — "Spogliato" (indexed / abstracted)
- Various notes collected by Stoldo Frescobaldi
- on the practice of Commerce concerning the kinds [of goods],
- weights, measures, value, and Exchange-rates (*ragguagli*) from one Marketplace
- to another.

---

**Interpretive note.** This is the descriptive title-page of the volume, written in a later (eighteenth-century) cataloguing hand rather than the original mercantesca. It identifies the book as No. 22 of a series, attributes the contents to Stoldo Frescobaldi, and summarizes the work as a merchant's reference manual covering commodities, weights, measures, values, and the exchange-rates (*ragguagli*) between marketplaces. The top note "1409" with "Comodo di Ritrovare questo Libro" likely flags an internal finding-aid reference; "Spogliato" indicates the volume's contents had been excerpted or indexed.
