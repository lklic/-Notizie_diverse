# Diplomatic Transcription — Frescobaldi Mercantile Notebook

**Shelf / digital identifier:** `32044103438974`
**Title (from front cover, f. [Ir]):** *Libro N° 22 Spogliato — Notizie diverse raccolte da [S]toldo Frescobaldi d[el]l'Esercizio della Mercatura circa i generi, pesi, misure, valore, e Ragguagli d'una Piazza all'altra*
**Owner / compiler (from f. 1r):** *Questo libro X di Stoldo di Lionardo Frescobaldi*
**Genre:** Merchant's reference book / *zibaldone mercantile* — notes on commodities, their qualities, weights, measures, monetary values, and exchange ("ragguagli") between trading places.
**Hand:** Florentine *mercantesca* commercial cursive, heavily abbreviated. The later cover note ("Libro N° 22…") is in an 18th-century archival hand.
**Language:** Italian / Florentine vernacular with Latin abbreviations and mercantile shorthand.

---

## Scope & reliability

This is a **diplomatic (semi-diplomatic) transcription**: it preserves the original spelling, word division, line breaks, abbreviations (expanded in *italics* within parentheses where confident), and page layout as far as Markdown allows.

⚠️ **Important caveat.** The manuscript is written in a dense, abraded, and highly abbreviated *mercantesca* hand. Many readings are uncertain. This transcription is a careful **best-effort reading**, not a definitive scholarly edition. It should be verified against the originals (and ideally against higher-resolution scans) by a trained paleographer before being used as an authoritative source.

## Editorial conventions

| Symbol | Meaning |

| `(word)` in *italics* | Editorial expansion of an abbreviation, e.g. `p(er)`, `s(ono)`. |
| `[word]` | Editorial reconstruction of letters lost to damage, trimming, or ink loss. |
| `[?]` | The preceding word/reading is uncertain. |
| `[…]` | Illegible passage (length roughly proportional to dots). |
| `[blank]` | Intentionally blank space, ruling, or unwritten line. |

| `|` | (Within notes) a column / vertical divider visible in the layout. |

- **Numbers, weights and currency** are transcribed as written. Common mercantile signs: `lb` / `libbre` (pounds), `on` / `once` (ounces), `s.` (soldi), `d.` (denari), `fl.`/`f.` (fiorini), `gr.` (grani / grossi). Where the scribe uses a symbol that cannot be reproduced, it is described in `[ ]`.
- Line breaks in the transcription follow line breaks in the manuscript.
- Page headings give the **image file number** and, where present, the **original folio number** written by the scribe (top outer corner).

## Contents at a glance

The notebook is, in effect, a Florentine merchant's *manuale di mercatura*. Its main blocks are:

1. **Wool & commodity gazetteers** (ff. 2–4) — qualities, weights and duties of wools and *spezierie*, town by town (Catalonia, Aragon, Valencia).
2. **Ragguagli — cross-market conversion tables** (ff. 4–9, and ff. 22–26) — weights, measures, money and prices of one marketplace against another (Barcelona as the pivot; Perpignan, Montpellier, Avignon, Paris, Bruges, Savona, Pisa, Florence, Granada, Palermo, Venice, Majorca/Minorca, Ibiza, Seville, then the Levant: Alexandria, Damascus, Cyprus, Constantinople).
3. **Cloth & dye references** (ff. 12–15) — manifests, grades and dyeing/silk price-lists.
4. **Coinage assays** (ff. 16) — gold and silver coin fineness tables.
5. **Genoa- and Florence-centred metrology & *cambio* tables** (ff. 18–22) — and a regional survey of Italy (Sardinia, Rome, Naples, Sicily, Bologna, Milan).
6. **Trade-fair calendars & bill-of-exchange usances** (ff. 26–27).
7. **Statutory texts** (ff. 28–29) — the Pisan customs *gabella* law (1470?).
8. **Maritime / galley memoranda** (ff. 30, 71) — galley wages, manning and provisioning.
9. **Blanks, flyleaves, binding** (end leaves and spine).

> Foliation note: the scribe's folio numbers in the stretch ff. 4–6 are inconsistent/repeated; uncertain folio labels are marked `[?]`. A late jump to "f. 71" appears at image 063 (the galley provisioning leaf).
