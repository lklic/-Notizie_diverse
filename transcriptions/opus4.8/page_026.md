**English translation.**

Top block — *"The goods loaded for Perpignan; first."*
- One bale loaded at Florence for Perpignan, namely: ½ *borsi*; 1 *sbradato*; 1 other; 1 *aguerno*; 1 cloth (*borse*) of turquoise; 1 *pepo*; and 1 *pagonazzo* (peacock-purple) of the finest (*dolpremo*). // Another bale: 1 *alestro* scarlet-cloth at ½ lb.; 1 brown-green *aguerno*; 1 turquoise *pagonazzo*; and a finest *pagonazzo*; 1 *sbradato* green; and 1 finest green.
- There are bales of cloths for Perpignan, of weight (lots) of 4, namely: 1 *narco*; 1 green *guarco*; 1 *alestro guarco*; and *aguerni*; 1 white; 1 scarlet; and 1 finest green.
- There is a bale of cloths of *Monaleca* of weight 4, namely + the backs (*dossi*); 1 *narco*; 1 green *guarco*; 1 white; 1 *alestro guarco*; that is, with *aguerni*.

Rubric — *"Tariff of goods at Perpignan, November … December, the year (of Christ) 1418; and first, … these are three quintals (hundredweight)."*

Left table, **Goods by the 3 quintals (in florins):** Pepper (*pape*) 180; boiled sugar (*amidori bollati*) 175; brazilwood (*verzini*) 112; *asnamo* cotton 35; Turkish cotton 25; candles 7½; copper (*rame*) [—]; ginger (*zingoro*) of 3 *cote* 30.

*Agrumera* (drugs/aromatics): ivory (*avorio*), Colombine 45; ivory *almuri* 35; *dischamoni* sulphur (*zolfo*) 2; clean refined alum 10; *nitro* alum, not refined 7; *ontory zona* 6; *orlate* 9; *ame* [—].

*Togole* (cloths/linens): Borgo linens, narrow 21 s.; Borgo linens, wide 24 s.; veils (*veli*) passing 16 a 16 s.; gum (*gomma*) 13 a 14 s.; *alegoj zenobani* 25 s.; *chamberi* of Ponte 24 s.

Right table, **By the pound (in soldi unless noted):** *Indacho bonghaddo* (Baghdad indigo) fl. 1 per lb.; cloves (*garofani*) 13 s.; *Non dalce* refried 3¼; *Non dotto* 24[?]; long pepper (*pepe lungho*) 8; *Doma* 6; fine galingale (*galinga fine*) 7; spikenard (*spigonardi*) 9; *comma gromata* (gum-) 14; fine scammony (*schomonea fine*) 20; broken camphor (*canfora disfatta*) 14; *boveana ipparte* 90; greasy pine-resin (*pinea zgrasso*) 14; fine *cilandino* 14; *romo paradisa* (grains of paradise?) 11.

Foot prose (right): Rhubarb (*rabarba*) … the tare turns, on resale, to a charge at Rome of s. ½ 4; … camphor (*champora*) of Germa, tare … of charge, sold at lb. 118 to 120; … *Spignj* (Spanish?) … the tare … turns at Florence to *stara* … ; … by the *staio* 2⅜ of exchange …

---

**Interpretive note.** A merchant memorandum recording (a) the contents of bales of Florentine cloth packed for shipment to Perpignan — itemizing dyed cloths (scarlet/*scarlattino*, peacock-purple/*pagonazzo*, turquoise, greens) by quality grade (*dolpremo*=finest, *sbradato*, *aguerno*) — and (b) a Perpignan price-tariff dated November–December 1418, divided into goods priced by the three-quintal (spices: pepper, sugar, brazilwood, cotton, ginger; an *agrumera* drug list; and a *togole* linen list) and goods priced by the pound (indigo, cloves, galingale, scammony, camphor, spikenard, etc.). The foot adds notes on tare allowances and resale conversions for rhubarb and camphor at Rome and at Florence. Several quality-terms and a few words in the dense top lines remain conjectural.
