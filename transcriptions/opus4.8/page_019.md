**Interpretive note.** Folio 9r is a three-part Alexandria–Catalonia trade memorandum. The first block is a *ragguaglio* converting Alexandria weights/measures (rotoli, *mena*, bezants) into Barcelona values for pepper and other goods, with notes on customs duty, tare and currency. The second block lists the *mercatantia*/spezierie traded at Alexandria — indigo (*indaco*), incense, camphor, sandalwood, cotton (raw and spun), saffron, cloves (*garofani*), zedoary, rhubarb(?), long pepper, cardamom, cinnamon (*cannella*), silk, cassia-fistula (*chassafistola*), ginger (*zenzero*), honey, dates (*dattori*), yarn (*stame*), tin (*stagno*), lead (*piombo*), soap (*sapone*) and pine-nuts/pigments. The third block is a graded packing/specification list for a chest of swords (*spade*) bound for Catalonia: quantities entered in the margin (70, 20, 25, 80, 10, 10, 33), qualities (broad-bladed, ridged/plain "schienite," gilt, engraved), and instructions about maker's marks/lettering on the blade imitating Venetian work and about thin steel — practical procurement notes for the Levant–western-Mediterranean arms and spice trade.
