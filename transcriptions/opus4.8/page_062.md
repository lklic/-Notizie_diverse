**Interpretive note.** This recto is not a commodity/ragguagli table but a **maritime / galley memorandum**: a note (opening *Nota dichome una galea...*) on how a galley is to be ordered, crewed and equipped, with all it requires. It lists the ship's officers and crew — the *soprastante* overseer and his deputy, the *comito* sailing-master and under-comito, a *consolio* council, a *siniscalco* steward — together with the specialist crew (*calafato* caulker, *maestro d'ascia* carpenter, *barbiere* surgeon, a *balestriere* crossbowman) and a complement of **18 *usinali*** making up the body of *compagnoni*. It also covers banners and pennons (*bandiere quadre*, *pennoni*), the battle-oath (*saramento da battaglia*), watch-keeping and unloading in port, taking on water ashore, and rigging (*vela*, *gherone*). The text is fast cursive prose, badly abraded, so most readings are insecure; the lower half of the leaf is blank.
