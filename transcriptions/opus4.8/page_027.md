**Interpretive note.** A two-column merchant price-list, headed by what appear to be the closing/dating lines of a commercial letter keyed to trade fairs and feast-days (Santa Chiara; Ognissanti / first of December). The body grades silk and cloth goods — "palmella" and "orsoglio/ogni" (silk grades) on the left, "panni" (cloths) on the right — distinguished by origin (Tolosa = Toulouse, Avignona = Avignon, Bologna, "labordo/laborda", "garamonte/argamonte", "ponte") and by quality/colour terms (chiara = light, mischi = mixed, azurry = azure, biggi = grey, biavo = blue, ranche, abisso), each priced in florins, with several values given as ranges (e.g. 15 e 16, 13 e 15). The script is heavily abraded and abbreviated, so many origin/grade epithets remain conjectural; the lower half of the leaf is blank.
