**Interpretive note.** A reference checklist of mercantile goods and *spezierie* (drugs/spices) grouped into three classes by the Florentine reckoning-weight under which the consignment is rated — goods reckoned in Florence at lb 138, at lb 92, and those sold "a libbra." The first list runs over textiles and raw materials (Turkish/Alexandrian cotton, Cyprus/Alexandria thread, gum arabic, Majorca/Minorca/Cotswold[?] wool, alum, tartar, ash, azure, tin, lead, iron, soap, sulphur, hides). The second and third are long apothecary inventories: several grades of ginger, cinnamon and sugar (di Malaga, damascene), incense, madder, cassia fistula, almonds, ivory, kermes, varnish, verdigris, mastic, orpiment, vitriol, saltpetre, sal ammoniac, sandalwood, zedoary, turpentine; then long pepper, cloves, nutmeg/mace, cubebs, camphor, carpo-/xylobalsam, castoreum, bdellium, rhubarb, galbanum, opopanax, tragacanth, several aloes (epatico, caballino), cardamom, chebulic myrobalans, dragon's blood, scammony, costus, spodium, several saffrons by origin, silks, and sheepskins/cordovan leather. The hand is abraded and heavily abbreviated; many drug and place names are given only tentatively.
