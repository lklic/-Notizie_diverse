**English translation.**

Top rubric: *"+ [...] of Genoa"* — list of Genoa goods: ultramarine azure / German azure / ambergris ("amber of whale") / pearls to ship(?) / spun gold of Genoa / spun silver of Genoa / [plus two further, illegible items].

Second rubric: *"+ Embroideries(?) of Genoa"* — English cloths / white cloths of Belforte(?) / and enamel(?), gum arabic.

Third rubric: *"+ Most common[ly] the payments are reckoned/exchanged at Genoa."*

The body is a Genoa exchange-usance ("ragguagli") list. Each "per …" entry tells how Genoa exchanges with a market; each "e da … a genoba" gives the reciprocal back to Genoa. Recurring formulas: *sicambia* = "is exchanged," *chontro a tornano* = "converts against," *sehondo temporali / sehonde il tempo* = "according to the season."

- At Genoa payments are made [...] of profit, worth [...] .
- **Pisa**: exchanges as it does, equally, being much [...] or at a price according to the season.
- **Florence**: similarly, and the same payments are made [there].
- *From Florence to Pisa / from Genoa*: exchanges [at the rate that] follows, against … 25 d. in gold(?).
- **Lucca**: exchanges as it does; likewise from where [it] is long(?) … of Genoa.
- **Ancona** to Genoa and from Genoa to Ancona, likewise, as above; payments(?) can be made.
- **Provence(?) and Rome**: and from Genoa … in those same parts, as above.
- **Naples / Gaeta / all Puglia**: exchanges in Genoa, 25 d. … against; registered(?) 60 which make 6, and the ounce is worth 7 to 8½ d., for each one according to the season.
- The same exchange holds for Genoese(?) and at the above-said ports/places by reckoning.
- **Palermo and all Sicily**: exchanges 1 against so many [units] of 12 … the bisanti of 1 are worth more or less; likewise the reverse to Genoa.
- **Famagosta and all the island of Cyprus**: exchanges 1 … 5¾ are worth one ducat, more or less; likewise the reverse to Genoa.
- **Alexandria**: exchanges 1 of Genoa against so many bisanti — 24 [carats] make one bisante, and the lira/carat of silver is worth 13 to 18 [d.] according to season. — *From Alexandria*: one bisante against so many carats of one bisante, silver at 13 to 18 according to season.
- **Damascus**: exchanges 1 against so many bisanti / against so many soldi of Genoa; dirhem(?) worth 18 to 19 fl.(?). — *From Damascus*: so many dirhem against one of Genoa.
- **Caffa**: exchanges 1 against so many aspers and a bisante(?); … so many aspers make one ducat(?). — Likewise the reverse from the said countries of Genoa.
- **Scio(?)/Mitilene and all the Archipelago**: exchanges as fixed.
- **Romagna**: exchanges as fixed, or in other grossi one less; [as at] Constantinople according to season … of Genoa. — *From Romania/Venice(?)*: exchanges in [grossi?] against others.
- **Bologna**: exchanges as it does, being better and worse according to the season. — *From Bologna*: likewise.
- **Milan**: exchanges as it does … fl. 32(?), which are always better; in money from Genoa 1 to 30 per cent(?) according to … the coinage shifting. — *From Milan*: likewise, fl. 32 imperial against [those] of Genoa.
- **Sciacca(?)/Sigmone**: exchanges as it does, in chamber [money?], sometimes better, sometimes worse.
- *From **Avignon(?)***: exchanges in chamber [money] against … 5 d. in gold(?).

---

**Interpretive note.** Folio 19r is the Genoa section of the notebook's exchange/usance compilation. After three short rubrics listing characteristic Genoese merchandise (ultramarine and German azure, ambergris, pearls, spun gold and silver thread, English cloth, gum arabic), it sets out, in paired entries, how Genoa's money and weights are "ragguagliati" against a sweep of Mediterranean markets — Pisa, Florence, Lucca, Ancona, Provence/Rome, Naples/Gaeta/Puglia, Palermo and Sicily, Cyprus (Famagosta), Alexandria, Damascus, Caffa, Pera/Constantinople, the Aegean (Mitilene/Archipelago), Romagna, Venice(?), Bologna, Milan and Avignon(?) — using units such as bisanti, carats, dirhem, aspers and ducati, and repeatedly noting that rates vary "secondo il tempo" (with the season). Several numeric readings and a few place- and unit-names are uncertain owing to abrasion; the place-names, the verb *sicambia*, the *a genoba/da genoba* formula and most numerals are reasonably secure.
