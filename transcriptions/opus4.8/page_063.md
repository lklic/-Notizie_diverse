**Interpretive note.** This page departs from the gazetteer/ragguagli matter of the surrounding manuscript: it is a short **provisioning and equipment checklist headed "Provedimento alla galea[?]" ("Provisioning for the galley")**, each item opened by the scribe's looping entry-initial and closed by a "need" formula (*al bisogno suo*, *qualche bisogno*, *facendo al maggio[r]*, "supplying as the greater need requires"). Legible items match a galley's stores and gear: *armi* (arms), *balestre* (crossbows, some "latine"), *l'albero* (the mast), *tavole dipinte*[?] (painted boards/pavises), wheat (*formento*, of Aragona[?]/Spain[?]), *vermiglio e bianco* (red and white wine[?]), a *botte d'aceto* (cask of vinegar), *formaggio* (cheese), *salma*/salt, *insalata* (greens), with references to Gaeta and to a *torre* (tower). The hand is rapid and badly abraded; the transcription above is a provisional reading carrying substantial uncertainty, and the lower half of the leaf is blank.
