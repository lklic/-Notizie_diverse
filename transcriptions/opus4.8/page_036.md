**Interpretive note.** A working merchant's memorandum on the cost of moving and reselling a commodity (grain reckoned by the *staio*, and/or wool) through the Western-Mediterranean trade network: customs and charges (*dispesa*), stowage (*allo stono*), freight (*nolo*), reworking/refining loss (*ghoverghatura/torghon*), and quality, with per-*staio* values in *lb* and *d.* for delivery "up to Empoli," "to Firenze," and "to Avignone," shipped via Barceloneta/Catalonia. A short cancelled word and the paler ink below mark a separate, later-added paragraph on cloth measurement (*larghezza* in *palmi*) relating Firenze, Pisa, and Venice. The hand is badly abraded and many forms (e.g. *torghon, ghoverghatura, asegua, durcha*) remain doubtful and are flagged as uncertain.
