**Interpretive note.** A *ragguagli* (exchange-and-trade) page comparing two western markets. The Montpellier ("monpolieri") block gives the cambio against Bologna and other places — reckoned in *fiorini*, *franchi*, *grossi* and *soldi* (ß) — and then how spices ("speza"), cloth/thread of gold ("drappi d'oro", "oro filato") and silver ("argento") are sold by the *libbra sottile* and converted to the *libbra grossa* (e.g. "torna in grossa lb 384"). The Barcelona ("barzalona") block similarly records exchange "per tutti luoghi" (Genoa[?], Bologna, etc.) in *reali*, *scudi d'oro* and *grossi*, cross-referencing Avignon, then how silverware/silver ("argentaria"), ivory ("avorio"), coral/goods and saffron ("zaffarano") are sold by weight (*once*, *libbre*), with saffron noted as fetching a premium. Place-names (Montpellier, Bologna, Barcelona, Florence, Aragona, Avignon) are reasonably secure; much descriptive and numerical detail is only partly legible.
