**Interpretive note.** A two-part *ragguagli* (exchange/weight-conversion) entry for the great northern markets. **Section 1 ("parigi" = Paris)** records that French payments are made in *franchi* (the gold franc, "il franco vale s. 16 di parigini"), how spices, silk and spun gold are sold by the pound and the local *cantaro/rotolo* converts to the Florentine pound (e.g. "= lb 169 / lb 160", silk "16¾ once"), with a comparison to another market (Flanders/Alexandria). It closes with the **wares of France** — fine cloths of **Brussels (borsella)** and **Montivilliers (monsuvolany)**, *brune*, Chartres, Provins wools, fine linens of Reims — and assorted spices and dyestuffs (*verzino colombino, cannella, gengiovo, gummi, nardi, zucchero*). **Section 2 ("Bruggia"/"Brenoggia" = Bruges)** values Florentine/Flemish gold coins — the **noble**, the **corona**, **gigli d'oro/scudi** and **franchi** — against the local *muda* money (grossi/carlini) and against the **London (Londra)** exchange, with notes on spice sales and weight conversions (400 lb Bruges = a *cantara* of 520; 100 lb Bruges = lb 137¼ Florence). The hand is heavily abbreviated and the right margin trimmed, so many coin/weight equivalences are read with reservation.
