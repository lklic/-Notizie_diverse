Subject: the weights/measures, entry-duty (gabella) and export charges of **Damasco** (Damascus) and how they ragguagliano (convert) with **Barzalona** (Barcelona). **English translation.**

**+ Damascus, at Barcelona**
- The *gintare* (kintar/cantaro) of Damascus is 100 *ruotoli* (rolls), at 12 once to the *colonna*, and 50 [...]; at the [...] it converts at Barcelona — the charges, not maintained, about 5 p(er cent) — and it converts to 17 lb 12 [...].
- The [...] charge is 1/6; the *ruotolo* makes at Barcelona 9 once [...]; [reckoned at] 24 carats and 70 sacks make a weight [...] 6½ make once, [reckoned] the 9th of March at Barcelona.
- The first charge — not carats — ¼ or ½ or ¾, and similar charges, as understood, count toward [it].
- [So many] units, ½, of March at Barcelona make one charge of Damascus.
- The *pinzo* makes 2 3/5 *palmi* at Barcelona.
- The appraisal/spread of Damascus is reckoned at 2½[?] at Barcelona.
- The *ruotolo* is of 5 once p(er cent), and the tare makes 4½ lb; and the *garra* (gross) at 5 p(er cent) makes 4½ lb. Every consignment of Damascus [goods] is tared 5 p(er cent), and the net is paper and twine.
- And take heed: small parcels do not pay the duty levied on Damascus goods, [on] entry and exit.
- The *gintar* (kintar) [of Barcelona/abovente] is greater by 1/5 than that of Damascus.

**+ Entry-gabella (customs duty) of Damascus, and other charges**
- One pays at Damascus on goods, on entry, 7¼ p(er cent), and the seal/purse of the *moro* (Moor's office?) and of Cyprus[?] 5 d(enari) per bale; and the duty of the inspector and of the weigher 7/4 per bale; brokerage 2 p(er cent), without counting the costs of the men employed; thus on loading, from 8 p(er cent).
- One reckons the p(er cent) on entry at Damascus per bale of 10 [...] in addition, of 1.
- One brings a [quantity] of Catalan [cloth] at the cost of [duties] at Damascus itself; sold, [it does] not... amounting in proportion to [...] at 20 p(er cent), etc.
- The customs office charges per bale 1¼ per cloth, and the cloths pass at the customs office of duties at 2 per bale; and protection (escort) of duties at Damascus from 24 to 30 *charach* (carats?).

**Interpretive note.** A three-part mercantile memorandum on trade between **Damasco** (Damascus) and **Barzalona** (Barcelona). The first block sets out the local weights and measures of Damascus — the *gintare/kintar* of 100 *ruotoli*, the *pinzo* equal to 2 3/5 *palmi* at Barcelona, the tare at 5 p(er cent), and conversions of weight (e.g. converting to 17 lb 12) — and notes that the kintar is larger than the corresponding standard by 1/5. The second block, under *Gabella d'intrata*, lists the entry customs at Damascus (c. 7¼ p(er cent) plus seal-fees, inspector and weigher duties, brokerage and handling). The third, under *Spese all'uscita*, itemises the export charges — weighing-fee, collectors' and weighers' duties, porterage/escort, brokerage, and the merchant's per-bale (per-head) payments. A practical reckoning for a Florentine handling Levantine goods through the western Mediterranean.
