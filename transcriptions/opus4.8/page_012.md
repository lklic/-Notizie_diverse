**English translation.**

**+ Savona with Barcelona**
- ※ 100 (units) of *chondom*(?) at Savona are 190 *rotoli grossi* in Barcelona, and the cargo charge is wont to be fl. 15 (and fl. 12 is asked) at Savona, therefore of the cargo...
- *Orentmaro*(?) at Savona is by *dubbi* about 1½ of *chondom*(?), which by *dubbi* is said to be 7½ in Barcelona; and it gains by the above first, as the spun-thread (*filato*) and the tow (*stoppa*) and similar goods work; and the other goods of Savona one acquires as that with which one has, toward d. 3½ profit.
- ※ A *chondom*(?) of *palere*(?) makes by the *charpeza*(?) per ⅔ ⅞ *rotoli* in Barcelona; they are ⅔ ¾ palms wide.
- ※ A *canna* of Savona makes ⅔ ⅕ *canne* of *verga* (rod-measure) in Barcelona.

**+ Pisa with Barcelona**
- ※ The lambs'-wool goods (*agnine*) with the charge in Pisa, up to sold, counting freight (*nolo*), and *in promtimto* by v(?) profit.
- ※ Now by the right method, with the charge in Rome, sold, not counting it is reckoned /·/ freight lb. per per cent ad per *quintale*(?); and s. 1⅕ of charge of the *quintale* of the wool.
- ※ When then it is bought in Pisa, at charge up to acquired for Barcelona, in s. ⅕ ¼ at the *lavile*(?), of freight lb. 2 and 2¼ at the *lavile*(?), of everything toward s.; at charge up to the place of the *quintale* of the wool, returns in Pisa, by the way of Florence lb. 140 and 142 net, per every load of cargo in hand sold by weight.
- ※ What is bought at *Balonga*(?) returns, the *conforuncha*(?) from *Balonga*(?), toward lb. 375.

**+ Florence with Barcelona**
- ※ One *canio*(?) of cloth(?) of Florence returns three palms in Barcelona.
- ※ 1⅕ lb. of Florence returns one lb. at Barcelona.
- ※ The *quintale* of the wool returns net in Florence lb. 140 and 142, thus to be reckoned.
- ※ 100 lb. of *marolicha*(?) (Mallorcan?) returns lb. 125 at Florence, gross; it has at charge of *anhaui*(?). When *marolicha* is sold at Florence with commission (*provisione*) and freight it is reckoned at g. 4(?) profit.
- ※ Cloths (*panni*) with the charge in Rome sold at Florence, with freight and commission, are reckoned net at fl. 1¼ per cent per cloth.
- ※ ...with charge up to sold in Florence, with freight / and commission / is reckoned at two s. of Florence per *quintale*.
- 〇 *Seta d'Almería*(?) (silk), bought at *Balonga*(?), at charge in Rome becomes in hand, with the *appozione*(?) (commission), d. 1 profit per 14 *plra*(?); and at Florence at charge in Rome sold with commission, d. 1 profit, not counting, is reckoned at 7¼ profit per lb.; and 100 lb. of *Balonga*(?) are in Florence lb. 105¼ net.

---

**Interpretive note.** Three commercial *ragguagli* (equivalence tables) linking the marketplaces of Savona, Pisa and Florence each with Barcelona. The entries convert weights and measures (*rotoli grossi*, *libbre*, *canne*, *palmi*, *quintali*, cargo *cantari*) between the centres and discuss the wool / lambs'-wool trade (*lana*, *agnine*), spun thread (*filato*) and tow (*stoppa*), cloth (*panni*) and silk (*seta d'Almería*[?]), together with the associated charges (*spesa*, *quintaro/gabella*), freight (*nolo*), commission (*provisione/proporzione*) and profit margins reckoned in *denari* and *fiorini per cento*. The recurring "Balonga"(?) appears to be a Catalan/Provençal buying locality. The faded mercantesca makes many internal numbers and verbs uncertain.
