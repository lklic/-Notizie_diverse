**English translation.**

**+ Majorca [Manolicha] by itself**

- At Majorca there come from Barbary [North Africa] "pay"(?) silks / roe(?) — from Burgundy, from Soule, from Bayonne(?), from Saluzzo(?), from Tabensi(?); they are of "Hungarian"(?) crimson(?) royal rose [quality], f(lorins) 33 per pound.

- From Alghada(?), from Aone, from Montfrança(?), from Tunis(?) — these silks are all coarse silks; they carry duty(?) of f. 31 per pound.
- At Majorca there come very large quantities of hides from Barbary.
- Hides of Tunis, from Soule, are good "of Orsona"(?); in the ordinary [run], for [each]? weighing about 24–25 the hundredweight, Barbary [weight], they carry f. 79–78 per hundredweight, fifths (quinti).

**Interpretive note.** A two-part Western-Mediterranean trading dossier. The upper block, headed "Manolicha p(er) se medesima" (Majorca by itself), is a gazetteer of the silks and especially the hides/leather ("chuoia") that arrive at Majorca from Barbary (North Africa — Tunis, Bona/Annaba, Algiers, Bougie) and from various European origins (Burgundy, Soule, Treviso, etc.), giving qualities, weights per centonaio/quintale, customs and florin prices reckoned in the Barbary system, with a short set of weight conversions (cantar/quintal) against Tortosa, Marseille and Barcelona. The lower block, headed "Lana d'minorgha" (wool of Minorca), describes the wools of Minorca — naming Ciutadella ("Cittadella"), the principal town — their grades by 100/200-quintal lots, and the conversion of the Minorcan quintale to "lb. 100 net" at Florence. Heavy abbreviation and abrasion leave many proper names and quality terms conjectural.
