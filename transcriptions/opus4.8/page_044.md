**English translation.**

**+ Messina (wool).** In Messina one weighed everything fine by the *cantaro sottile* (fine hundredweight), weighing every merchandise, except that coarse goods (are weighed by) the said (coarse) *cantaro*, and always for all other matters concerning wool. Likewise, every coarse good by the coarse *cantaro*, which is larger than the fine one by e° 10 (a tenth). The *marco* of silver of Messina equals (the standard) at ?Boietta and in all that country; it converts in Florence to 7 oz 12 d.; it converts in Genoa to 8 oz 1 d. (≈ 8 oz at Messina).

**+ Calabria.** In Calabria, plenty of grain and other cereals (biade), fine wool (trafine), cheeses, ?juniper in salt, and tuna. In Malta: citron-fruit (citrea), cottons. In Palermo: citrons, sugar, kermes-grain (grane), and tuna. There is throughout all Calabria: cloth of not very fine sort, fustians, ?armoy, oil and wine, and Milanese (goods), armour; spices not many.

**+ Bologna.** In Bologna ducats would be reckoned (abmoçena) as in Florence, Milan, (and) the Bolognese, and from these they make settlements (spacciamenti). For Milan it is reckoned (sirsombra) that the count at *cassa* is 32 d. in imperials per lira; and from Milan to Bologna, reckoned likewise. For ?Bologna it is reckoned that the count at *cassa* in *denari* of ?Cremona, and from ?Bologna (vice versa). For Paris it is reckoned that the count at *cassa* in francs; and from Paris to Bologna likewise. For Bruges it is reckoned that the count is in *grossi* (di) ?Tournai per ?barla, grossi 36 d. 90, and one ?temporal grosso; and from Bruges to Bologna, grossi counted at *cassa*. Bologna ?sends to ?Arentinoro. For Rome 36 d. in Bologna convert to (the money) of ?Modena, grossi 35 d. The ?largoeba of Bologna converts in Florence to ?3½ (per) staio. The *marco* of silver in Bologna converts in Florence to 8 oz 11 d.; the said *marco* in Bologna converts in Modena to 8 oz 2 d.

**+ Milan (Melamo).** In Milan there are moneys of several values struck by the past lords, each at its proper ?casanal value; with settlements of exchange and ?ramichotonche, which (run at) 32 d. in imperials. (One) governs with ?denenbo that Florence and Milan in Bologna are reckoned (abmoçena) — ?Lucca, Pisa, ?Perugia, ?Baghono — at 32 and 38 imperials, ?ordinary terms. For every part it is reckoned that at *cassa* per lira the count is in the money on which the settlements are founded, in (each) place; there are two reckonings.

**Interpretive note.** A *ragguagli*/usances page treating four Italian marketplaces. The Messina and Calabria/Palermo/Malta entries are commodity gazetteers (wool weighing by the fine and coarse *cantaro*, grain, tuna, citron, sugar, kermes-grain, cloth, fustians, oil, wine, armour, spices) plus the silver-*marco* equivalences Messina–Florence–Genoa. The Bologna and Milan blocks are exchange notes describing how ducats and local moneys are reckoned (sirsombra/abmoçena) and settled (spacciamenti) between Bologna, Milan, Florence, ?Cremona, Paris, Bruges (Tournai grossi), Rome and Modena, with the silver-marco conversion Bologna–Florence–Modena. Several technical verbs and the long right-running rate lines are abraded and read uncertainly.
