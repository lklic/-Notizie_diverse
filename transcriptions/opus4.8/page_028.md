**Interpretive note.** This is a price-list / gazetteer of English and French wools (*lane*), cloths (*panni*), skins (*pelli*) and dyestuffs, opening with a header "for the merchant, of England and France." The left column catalogues English wools by region (apparently Worcester, etc.) and English cloths and skins with values in florins per piece or per hundredweight; the right column ("In Francia") lists French cloths each rated by how many *rotoli* (weight units) a piece runs and its florin value, then quotes rock and ground alum, sulphur (12 fl/migliaio) and a closing note describing the contents of a bale of Damascene/oriental and grey cloths and how they sell. Many place- and cloth-names are heavily abbreviated and abraded; readings of proper names are tentative.
