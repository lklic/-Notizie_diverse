**English translation.**

> *(Heading)* **+ Of the campo (district) of [d']Iranzo / "direnza".**

The page is a list of wool-producing localities of Spain — Castile, Aragon, Catalonia and Valencia — grouped by region, each cluster of place-names closing with a verdict on the wool. Because the toponyms were heard and re-spelled by a Tuscan scribe and the ink is abraded, most are transcribed as written and marked uncertain.

- **First block** (places of the *campo di Iranzo[?]*): a string of localities ending "…and the washed wool (*alla seura*) of the campo di Iranzo are common/middling wools."
- **Second block** (the Castile / Aragon side — incl. *Castiglia* = Castile, *Montalbán*, *Monzón[?]*, *Puerto/Oviedo[?]*): "all these places are middling for the washed wool; and as many as are in the campo di Iranzo [are] good wools."
- **Third block** (Catalonia — *Villafranca, Vall, Gualba, Tortosa[?]…*): "[these] are all places of the [district] of Valencia[?] (*d'Alda di Varença*) and are all very fine wool (*bonissima lana*)."
- **Fourth block** (*Valeua…, aluona, …*): "[these] are of the campo di Iranzo [and] are all good wools — common[?] of the Levante[?]."
- **Fifth block** (*[P]unto Sombel, Fonte d'Sallo / d'Saluno, Bugia[?]*): "[these] are places of [the district of] Vizcaya[?]; … in the watercourses[?], and are not so good a wool to wash."
- **Sixth block** (*Traveas, Monte Negro, Villabella, …*): "in the campo di [Calatrava?] (*galsporite*) [these] are common[?] wool and not very good."
- **Three columns** (further localities): col. B footed "[these] are very good[?] wools"; col. C ends "…and [they] are good wools."
- **Final running list** (*Ynssuppa, …, Saragozza[?]=soraoghosa, …, Tortona[?]=tortonda, …, cambll*): "[these] are fine — as [the] confines of the *termine* of [the region]."
- **Closing summary line:** "[These] are all good places, and the good wools are of the campo di Lomença[?] and of the wash (*della seura*)."

*(This page records no weights, money, dates or exchange rates — it is a purely qualitative wool-source gazetteer. Glosses: "alla seura / della seura" = washed wool; "lane" = wools; quality terms — buone = good, bonissima = very fine/best, fine = fine, ghommali/ghomma/comuni = common/middling, "non molto buone" = not very good.)*

---

**Interpretive note.** A wool gazetteer (*lane di Spagna*): the scribe catalogues by district ("d'l champo di…") dozens of wool-producing towns of the Iberian peninsula — Castile, Aragon, Catalonia, Valencia and Vizcaya[?] — each long slash-separated roster of place-names closed with a verdict on the wool (best / good / common / not very good) and a note on whether it is washed (*alla seura*). A three-column keyed list in the middle adds further localities with their own verdicts, and a closing line summarises that all listed places are good-wool districts of "the Campo and the wash." Several recognizable names survive (Castiglia=Castile, Montalbán, Monzón[?], Villafranca, Saragozza[?]=Zaragoza, Tortona/Tortosa[?]), but because the names are recorded phonetically and heavily abbreviated, nearly every reading is tentative.
