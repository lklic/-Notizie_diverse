**English translation.** *(Heavily abbreviated and abraded statute text; the rendering below is approximate, with doubtful passages flagged.)*

- *(continuing from previous folio)* …of the said merchandise: it shall always be obliged (pledged) to the Commune of Florence for the due customs-duty (gabella), and likewise meanwhile to the lordship[?], until full payment.

- **§9** — In the matter where [goods] are conveyed[?] from the marketplace, or from any other country to which they answer[?], through any land or place of the Commune of Florence, in which the Commune has tax-burden[?] or lordship: for transit, to recover it and send it out of the territory belonging to the Commune of Florence, without stopping, let there be paid the customs by weight of the said pennies of silver[?]; insofar as [it goes] to the first passing-merchant… by the Commune of Florence: gold florins of duty (ghatto) for everything that pays[?] the duty[?], when it is conducted; to bring it into Florence, [from] countries beyond, [there are due] florins of duty as ordained by the customs-house of the gates of Florence, when it is conducted; to bring it into Pisa, [from] countries within or beyond, florins of duty as ordained by the customs-house of the gates of Pisa, when it is conducted; to bring it into any other place subject to the Commune of Florence — and except[?] between Florence and Pisa — let there be paid what the ordinances now in force prescribe; … likewise any other passing-toll or gabella, whatever the time, and however ordained, that shall be collected, whether by the Commune of Florence or by others… and by the first passing-merchant let the receipt (bulletta) be made of the payment of the duty florins, in which place it is to be conducted.

- **§10** — In the matter where, by the Commune of Florence, all the salt shall be bought up[?] that shall be… by any notary[?]… as merchandise that answers to[?] Pisa: which salt shall be… from the parts of Provence, or of Catalonia, or [from] the Levant — understanding "Levant" as everything from M[od]one[?] (Modon) upward toward the Levant; for this reason the salt of one part, or [salt of] island[?], six per staio of weight of 80 pounds; whereas that of the Levant, at the same weight, nine per staio, delivered at the expense of the seller in Pisa between the two bridges (fra' due ponti), bounded[?] by one man[?]… conducted between the said two bridges; and to this the said Commune is bound to be understood obliged, without any other pact or contract than this.

- **§11** — Except[?] that the salt that is poured out[?], in storing it[?], that has not at least half a staio[?]… as merchandise that answers within Pisa; or if it be ill-cleaned and not suitable, the Commune is not bound to buy it… that immediately the salt be conducted to the two bridges… [credited?] to the creditor, to the lord, to that [office], to that quarter[?] which, immediately the man being finished[?], by other… by pronouncement or deliberation, that [the manager?] of the salt-gabella, of whatever sum of money, shall be bound to pay the said credit[?]; and his[?] procurator[?], or whoever should have[?] the said credit, [to him] it be credited[?].

**Interpretive note.** Folio 29r carries a continuous vernacular statute/ordinance text (not the merchant's own commodity memoranda), copied into the book with numbered paragraphs (8–11) in the left margin. It regulates the *gabella delle porte* (gate-customs) and the *gabella del sale* (salt monopoly/tax) of the Commune of Florence, with Pisa as the chief destination: how merchandise remains pledged to the Commune for the duty until full payment; the duty in gold florins (ghatto) on goods in transit and at the gate-customs of Florence and Pisa, evidenced by a receipt (bulletta); and the Commune's monopoly purchase of salt arriving from Provence, Catalonia and the Levant — the Levant defined as everything "da M[od]one in su" (from Modon/Methoni upward) — priced per *staio* of 80 *libbre* (6 for one source, 9 for Levant salt), delivered at the seller's expense at Pisa "between the two bridges," with quality conditions. Much of the abbreviated wording is materially abraded and doubtful (see `[?]`).
