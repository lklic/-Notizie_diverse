**Interpretive note.** A merchant's market memorandum (folio 10r) on the trade of *Romania* and Constantinople (Pera): first a list of imports that sell well there — grain, "chario", linens, Florentine (San Martino) and Catalan/Perpignan cloths, saffron, quicksilver, tin, white soap (fl. 14), with bale-packing instructions; then a rubric "trasone di paesi sopradetti" listing return cargoes/exports — wax, buffalo and camel hides, several grades of cotton, rhubarb, scammony, pepper, pearls and gems; then a rubric on goods that also come via Naples (cottons, ginger, cassia, cinnamon, indigo, camlets), closing with sailing advice that the ship for Romania should leave about mid-February (at latest via Catalonia in March) and a tare/freight note of 140 lb. The hand is heavily abbreviated and abraded; many readings remain uncertain.
