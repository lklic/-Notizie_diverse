**English translation.**

Heading: *Praise to Christ, and to Florence, worthy [...] for the dyeing.*

**Woolen cloths of England, white:** four [are worth] from fl. 91⅛–93 the cloth // the *sferzonda* [a grade], reckoned fl. 94⅛–96 // the *taya* [grade], reckoned fl. 36⅛–38.

*Headed: "+ tintilani [coloured wools] of San Matteo":* Of Perlugio [Perpignan?] fl. 31⅛–33 the cloth // of San Matteo fl. 27⅛–29 the cloth // of Sferzona fl. 22⅛–25 the cloth. *They are sorted [for/from] Pisa; thus they are sferzonde.*

**They are valued by colour,** the above-said cloths according to the colours as we shall say below:
- 1 azure fl. 8 by colour / wanting it dark green, red[ground] fl. 10
- 1 sky-blue (cilestro) fl. 4 / wanting it dark green, red fl. 6 / wanting it peacock-purple (paghonazzo) fl. 9
- 1 *ebradato* (first grade) fl. 3 / green fl. 5 / peacock-purple fl. 8½
- 1 *ebradato* (other grade) fl. 2½ / green fl. 4 / peacock-purple fl. 8
- 1 turchino dyed twice fl. 2 / green fl. 4 / peacock-purple fl. 7½
- 1 turchino [light] fl. 1½ / green fl. 3½ / peacock-purple fl. 6½
- 1 scarlet without crimson fl. 3 / with crimson fl. 5
- 1 *ghupo* from fl. 8⅛–9
- 1 *tane* (tawny) fl. 3 / and peacock-purple fl. 5⅛–7½
- 1 *sharofanato* (saffron-coloured) fl. 3 / and peacock-purple fl. 4
- 1 white cloth washed of fine flax fl. 3 / of wool of San Matteo fl. 1

*As stated above: every cloth of [eba] fl. 2 and so much more, in proportion. Reds for "sguardo" [display/sample] that are made of [striped pieces?] / azures / of "biada" / and turchini / and of peacock-purple fl. 5¼, and so much more in proportion, red for display.*

**+ Stametti (worsteds) of San Matteo:** Of Perlugio from fl. 26⅛–28 // of San Matteo fl. 22⅛–24 // Sferzona fl. 17⅛–19 // *tanezi* fl. 14⅛–15.

Red [trimming?] of the scarlet fl. 2¼–3¾ // and azure [...] fl. ⅔ // sky-blue fl. 2½ // *sbradato* fl. 1¾ // *ghupo* fl. ⅗–⅙.

*On the drapery of Florence one may say that [the goods are] poorly assorted [...]; these at the present month [...].*

**Silks (zetani):**
- Crimson *zetani*, velveted and brocaded with gold — this cannot be stated; [...] Burgundy fl. 8 the pound, more or less according to the goods.
- Green *zetani* brocaded with gold fl. 5⅛–6 the pound // and black *zetani* brocaded with gold fl. 5 the pound.
- *Zetani* with crimson ground velveted in black, and white fl. 3⅓ the pound.
- Black *zetani* velveted fl. 2⅓ the pound // and velveted black fl. 1½ the pound.
- Crimson plain velvet fl. 3¼ the pound // and *damaschino* of grain (scarlet) fl. 2½⅛⅓ the pound.
- Black and [bardo] *damaschino* fl. 2⅛ the pound // and Alexandrine *damaschino* fl. 2⅛ the pound.
- Plain crimson *zetani* fl. 16 the pound // and plain azure *zetani* fl. 8 the pound.
- Black plain *zetani* fl. 6⅓ the pound.
- + Black taffeta of Florence fl. 6⅔ the pound // and taffeta of grain (scarlet) of Florence fl. 9 the pound.
- A *tena* [cloth] of Rubomah [?], white, of 15 pounds, is worth fl. 43⅛–44 the piece.
- + *tena* of Rubomah, crimson, fl. 44 the piece.
- *zperrah* [?] crimson and white fl. 38⅛–39 the piece.

**Interpretive note.** This page is a price-and-quality reference for textiles, in two parts: (1) finished woolen cloths and coloured wools ("tintilani") and worsteds ("stametti") of San Matteo and other origins (Perlugio, Sferzona), priced in florins per cloth and graded by dye-colour (azurro, cilestro, turchino, paghonazzo, sharlattino with/without crimson, tane, etc.); and (2) Florentine silks — brocaded and velveted *zetani*, *damaschini*, plain velvets, taffeta, and *tena*/*zperrah* — priced in florins per pound or per piece. Several silk lines are struck through, suggesting price revisions. The opening invocation dedicates the section to dyeing.
