**English translation.**

**+ Sardinia**
- Goods of Sardinia: lead, alum, hides/skins, coarse wools, plenty [and] coarse; likewise cheese, hides(?) of salted pig, like *chanali* and other things.
- And one exports some spices (*spezerie*) of the sea (*dimarine*); minor goods, oddments(?) of money.

**+ of Rome**
- At Rome there run [the] fl. (florins) of the Chamber, *fiorini*; fl. of the Chamber ..., money.
- Naples exchanges at fl. 1 of the Chamber against [its] *carlini*(?); in like manner the exchange is rated.
- Milan exchanges at fl. 1 of the Chamber against fl. 32, *imperiali*(?) ... ; and from Milan to/at Rome the same.
- Florence / Siena / Lucca / Spoleto(?) exchange at the fl. of the Chamber against the fl. of Scarperia(?).
- ...settled, to the *fine* (account closing).

**+ Naples and Gaeta**
- At Naples their money is reckoned by weight, by exchange and by coin; one understands fl. 1 [as the rate]; they exchange for every part: an ounce reckons against the coin of that land — the *carlini*(?) are [n] *tarì*, and fl. ... *tarì*, and 20 *grani*; and 30 *tarì* make an *oncia* of fl. 6.
- Timber of the Kingdom converts by weight of Pisa(?) ... to Florence; a Neapolitan *botte* (cask) ... ; they convert in Florence at ½ of the *fiordini*(?).
- And likewise throughout the whole Kingdom they sell goods by the *canna* measure: a Roman *canna* of 8 *palmi* converts in Florence ... ; ... and more, by the *braccio* of Holland(?) of Florence, fl. 9¼, the said *palmi*.
- A *botte* measure of Naples converts to 7 ... of Aronova(?).
- A *botte* measure of Naples holds 10 of the [measure] of Aronova(?).
- A *botte* by the *palmo* of Naples holds 44½ ... fl. 5 of Barcelona.
- 100 *[libbre]* "of the Roman weight"(?) of Barcelona convert in Naples to lb 16.6(?).
- Goods of Naples to draw out: there are plenty — *nocelle* (hazelnuts), chestnuts, with ... ; and one exports throughout all Apulia plenty of *dragone*(?) goods and spices, sea-goods, ... to Milan.
- Goods of Calabria: wine in plenty, ..., wheat (*formento*) / ... ; *avena* (oats) salted, and fruit ...; and one exports in Calabria some sea-goods.
- By the *palmo* of Apulia it converts ... reckoned at ⅓ ¾.

---

**Interpretive note.** A regional commodity-and-exchange survey continuing the *cambio* tables of the surrounding leaves, under three rubrics. **Sardinia** lists exports (lead, alum, hides, coarse wool, cheese, salt pork, sea-spices). **Rome** records money-changing relationships for the papal *fiorino di camera* against Naples, Milan, and the Florence/Siena/Lucca/Spoleto group. The longer **Naples & Gaeta** section sets out the local coinage (tarì, grani, oncia reckoned in florins), measures (canna, botte, palmo) and their conversions to Pisa, Florence, Rome and Barcelona, and ends with the chief exports of the Kingdom, Apulia and Calabria (hazelnuts, chestnuts, wine, wheat, oats, fruit, spices).
