**English translation.**

The page is a comparative cargo-and-price register: three columns of ※ *charatio* (ratings/valuations) listing the same eastern wares as carried by galleys arriving in 1419, several "come from Barbary" and dated December.

**Column 1 (rubric "Charatio of a galley from Barbary, one lot, September 1419," then "for Bengiara[?]/Bruges"):** pepper 176 (fl); ginger of all sorts 274; cinnamon 101; fine ginger 32; "gomenzina"/mezzina ginger 4; gum/nutmeg 8 (per hundred); raisins (uve passe) 1; dates 10; capers 1; zedoary 4; "non" 15; aloe 12; "ate" bales 6; galangal 4; "fani" ginger 58; picked pepper (aguinte) 126; pressed camphor 7; "ie chomo" pressed 2; indigo 7; ginger in baskets/bales 25; ginger in bales 2; camphor 1; aloe 40 (in barrels/bales).

*Sub-rubric "for l'India/l'Ondra":* pepper 182; ginger of all sorts 52; cinnamon in colli (loads) 26; cloves 25; "None" 2; "eta" bales 1; "fani" ginger 12; gum in colli 2; raisins 76; green ginger 81; picked pepper 39; incense 5; benzoin(?) 6; "boli" gum 61.

**Column 2 (rubric "for Ghada[s][?]"):** lacca 21; ginger of all sorts 12; brazilwood(?) 9; sugar/"doara" 8; fine cloves 16; green ginger 1; cinnamon 9; picked pepper 21; pepper 12; "gergumento" 5; indigo 11; "None" 1.

*Sub-rubric "for ... liria":* lacca 22; pepper 15; copper/fume(?) 7000 lb; brazilwood fine 4; picked pepper 1; saffron 2; cloves 2; "gergumento" 2; "cengino" 2.

*Sub-rubric "Charatio of certain goods come to Como[?] from Bar[u]antk[?], December 1419":* pepper in colli 1200; ginger of all sorts 2170; picked pepper 469; cloves 22; cinnamon/"cingha" 102; gum 4; "None gino" 4; indigo 4; benzoin 22; "eta" 6; pearl 19; "fumfari" returns within (?) at 12 per chu(?).

**Column 3 (rubric "Charatio of the cargo[?] from Barbary, of Venice[?], come in the year 1419, December"):** ginger however-much; cloves 21; nutmeg 102; "None" 17; cinnamon 27; indigo 18; lacca 90; benzoin 12; picked pepper 109; incense 4; "dargumento" 1; "cengino" 107; green ginger in spikes 192; alum 663; cottons 260.

*Sub-rubric "Charatio of how many goods of the spice(s)[?] come the same year, at Venice":* pepper 420; ginger of all sorts 209; cinnamon 107; picked pepper 12; "borsano" (benzoin) 6; incense 12; indigo 18; "denti barnoro" (ivory tusks?) 13; "polum chomthon" 50; of the cottons 7 or 20, "abisonh".

*Sub-rubric "cotton":* "Beta" (Beirut?) cotton 68 fondi; Rome 16; "cengino" 3; "soni" 4; Mantua 4.

*Sub-rubric "ghoesta[?] of the above-said":* "Beta" 59 fondi; Rome 15; wax 22.

**Interpretive note.** Folio 17r is a merchant's comparative cargo-and-price register: three columns of ※ *charatio*/ragguagli recording the staple eastern wares of incoming 1419 galleys (several "venuta d'Barbar[ia]" and dated December) and their valuations at different markets. The recurring goods are *pepe* (pepper, plain and *aguinte* = picked), *gengiovo* (ginger of all sorts, fine, green), *channella* (cinnamon), *garofani* (cloves), *indacho* (indigo), *lacca*, *camfora*, *incenso*, *zafferano*, *verzino* (brazilwood), *borsamini* (benzoin), *noce moscade*, *aloe*, *galangal*, *denti d'avorio* (ivory), *perle*, *alume*, *cotone* (Beta/Beirut, Rome, Mantua) and *seta/cera*, with quantities/values in florins, "c°", "r°", lb and fondi. Several rubric place-names and a few commodity terms are abraded and marked doubtful.
