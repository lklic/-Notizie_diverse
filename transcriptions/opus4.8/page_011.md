**Interpretive note.** This page is a sequence of five short ragguagli (metrological and monetary exchange notes), each pairing a marketplace or commodity with Barcelona: Avignon, "Pavoli" (Parigi/cloth?), hides/leather (cuoia), Cremona[?], and Bologna. It records how local weights (libbra, quintale, marco, arroba), lengths (palmi, cavezzi, rove) and money (grossi, denari) convert into Barcelona equivalents, with notes on transport charges (spesa) via Montpellier and on commodities such as Milanese white fustians and hides. The heavily abbreviated mercantesca hand leaves several technical unit-names (lunga, grappera, caviçi) and a couple of headings uncertain.
